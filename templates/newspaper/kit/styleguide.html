Do and Don't

Do

  • Titles state the finding in a few words.
  • Group with rules, space and tables.
  • Tabular figures with a source line.
  • Colour only for alarms, always with a word and a shape.
  • Values change without animation; only hover and focus move.

Don't

  • Cards, KPI tiles, pill chips, icons in coloured boxes.
  • Gradients, block shadows, radius everywhere.
  • Donuts, gauges, count-ups, fade-ins, uppercase eyebrows.
  • Red for every decrease.
  • A client's brand colours as the page palette.
