11Charts and the drawing

12Do and don't

Do

  • Put the unit beside every figure and the source mark on the same line.
  • Separate with rules and space. The page is a sheet, not a stack of boxes.
  • Give each view one primary chart and one headline figure.
  • Say a status with a shape and a word.
  • Draw comparisons in grey and the measured series in the mark.

Don't

  • Wrap a block in a bordered, tinted or shadowed card.
  • Stack shares that do not add up, or average two sources into one.
  • Mute text with opacity. Use the measured inks.
  • Use the mark for decoration. It means active, nothing else.
  • Set any text below 14 px on a screen or 22 px on a slide.
